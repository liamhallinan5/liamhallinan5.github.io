Experience

2025 – Dec 2026

Research Associate
Department of Engineering, University of Cambridge

Control of inverter-based grids, in the Control Group, on a project funded by an ERC Proof-of-Concept grant.

  • Developed a decentralised framework certifying the stability of AC power grids from local subsystem analysis alone, removing the need to analyse a model of the full network and enabling plug-and-play connection of new devices.
  • Extended the framework to subsystem representations that are themselves unstable — an inherent obstacle to applying decentralised stability methods to realistic grids.
  • Two journal papers under review at IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control; conference paper accepted at IEEE CDC 2026.
  • Presented to a mixed academic and industry audience at the ACE & UKACC Control Conference 2026. Presenting at the IEEE Conference on Decision and Control (CDC) in Dec 2026.
